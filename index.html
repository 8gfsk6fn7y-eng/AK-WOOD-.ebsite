About AK WOOD

AK WOOD is a custom woodworking atelier based in Prague.

Each project is approached individually with attention to detail, materials and long-term functionality. From the first consultation to the final installation, Adam Krejčíř handles the work personally to ensure quality and precision.

AK WOOD is founded and operated by Adam Krejčíř.

The workshop is based in Praha 6 - Bubeneč, with projects across Prague and nearby areas.

Our Process

  1. Consultation and measurements
  2. Design and quotation
  3. Production
  4. Installation

Who custom production is for

Custom production is ideal for clients looking for furniture tailored to a specific space, quality materials and long-term durability.
